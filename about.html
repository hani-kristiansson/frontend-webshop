About Us

Hi! My name is Hani Kristiansson, and together with my husband Henrik, we founded this company in 2025.

At the moment, we operate solely through our website, and our warehouse is based in Sundbyberg. What started as a simple homework project for my frontend course soon turned into a real business idea!

After building the website, I realized I could truly utilize it, and that's how By Hani was born. Everything you see here is carefully handpicked by me. I hope our selection of products brings joy and enriches your daily life.

Thank you for visiting, and I hope you find something you love!
